# Community code of conduct

Screen welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Treat people with respect, assume good intent, give useful technical feedback, and accept corrections thoughtfully.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, doxxing, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or block accounts. Decisions should be proportionate and consistent; conflicts involving a maintainer should be reviewed by another available maintainer.

Report public violations with GitHub's reporting tools. If a private channel is needed, open a minimal issue requesting a private conduct contact without publishing sensitive details. Maintainers will arrange a channel when available.
